About Our Clinic

Where Artistry Meets Dental Science

Suyog Dental was founded with a singular mission: to make premium dental care accessible, comfortable, and transformative. Located in the heart of Kathmandu, we combine the latest dental technology with a warm, spa-like environment.

Our internationally trained team of specialists is dedicated to crafting beautiful, healthy smiles. We believe every patient deserves a personalized treatment experience that respects their time, budget, and dental goals.

FAQ

Got Questions? We Have Answers

Professional whitening at Suyog Dental is completely safe when performed by our trained dentists. Results typically last 12–24 months depending on diet and lifestyle. We provide take-home maintenance kits to prolong your results.

Implant surgery is performed under local anesthesia — no pain during the procedure. Mild discomfort for 3–5 days after is manageable with over-the-counter medication. Full osseointegration takes 3–6 months, after which the permanent crown is placed.

Metal braces from NPR 45,000, ceramic from NPR 65,000. Invisalign from NPR 90,000 for minor corrections to NPR 1,80,000 for comprehensive treatment. EMI payment options available for all orthodontic treatments.

Absolutely. We specialize in anxiety-free dentistry. We offer nitrous oxide (laughing gas) for mild anxiety, plus oral sedation for moderate-severe cases. Our staff are trained in anxiety management and clear communication throughout all procedures.

Every 6 months for routine checkup and cleaning. Patients with gum disease or ongoing orthodontic treatment may need visits every 3–4 months. Children should have their first visit by their first birthday or when the first tooth appears.

Yes — we work with major providers in Nepal including Shikhar Insurance, Nepal Insurance, and several corporate health plans. We also help with international insurance documentation for expat patients, and offer 0% EMI for uncovered treatments.
